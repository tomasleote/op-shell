about me
========

Tomas Leote Falcao
Software developer, 24, based in Lisbon.


Who I am
--------
I like taking ideas and making them work, whether that means building
software and websites from scratch or solving problems that did not have
a solution yet. I bring a mix of technical skill, an entrepreneurial
mindset, and a desire to work in international, fast-moving teams.

I am currently a Junior Software Developer at Minsait, on the Low Code
and Innovation team, where I work on full-stack projects for several
clients using low-code tools such as OutSystems, making sure the
applications meet web accessibility standards.

Before that I did internships at Avodah Creatives (frontend in React and
Tailwind), at Hospital Lusiadas (Vue and .NET, full-stack), and at
Klippa, in the Netherlands, where I did my thesis building analytics
tools with Angular, GraphQL and Highcharts.


Beyond the code
---------------
I founded Project Umbra, a music events company in Groningen, focused on
low-cost events for the international community and on giving a stage to
local talent in the electronic music scene. We ran more than 20 events,
with up to 700 people each.

In my free time I surf and go to the beach, watch football, discover new
films and music, and I DJ.

Why a shell in the browser
--------------------------
I wanted to show a low-level project (C, memory management, processes) in
a way that anyone could try without installing anything. The solution was
to compile the same C to WebAssembly. Read "cat projects/op-shell.txt"
for the technical details.
